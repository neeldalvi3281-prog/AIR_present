# 25. ROTATION FRAME-TO-FRAME MATH

Where possible, use a signed angular delta based on vector relationships:

```text
dtheta =
atan2(
    cross(previous_axis, current_axis),
    dot(previous_axis, current_axis)
)
```

instead of expensive or fragile repeated absolute-angle conversions.

This naturally handles small directional changes and avoids ±180° subtraction problems.

Keep the existing mirror convention consistent.

Do not introduce multiple sign corrections.
